CLINICAL LOGIC AND DATA INTEGRITY JUSTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

This document outlines the clinical invariants, mathematical checks, 
and data leakage prevention strategies applied during the data 
preprocessing phase (via the `apply_clinical_logic` function).

1. PAIRWISE CONTRADICTIONS (LOGICAL ERRORS)
These checks identify variables that are mutually exclusive in a 
valid clinical reality. In cases of conflict (e.g., both flags = 1), 
the values are nullified (set to NaN) as they represent irresolvable 
data-entry errors.

  1.1. Weight Invariant (Pre-pregnancy vs. Admission)
       Clinical logic dictates that a patient's pre-pregnancy weight 
       must be less than or equal to their weight at admission for 
       delivery, accounting for gestational weight gain. 

  1.2. Amniotic Fluid Invariant (Polyhydramnios vs. Oligohydramnios)
       A patient cannot present with both excessive amniotic fluid 
       (polyhydramnios) and insufficient amniotic fluid 
       (oligohydramnios) during the same pregnancy. 

  1.3. Hypertension Type Invariant
       Based on ICD-9 coding conventions, chronic hypertension 
       (pre-existing) and gestational hypertension (pregnancy-induced) 
       are mutually exclusive primary classifications. 

  1.4. Delivery Pathway Invariant (Planned CS vs. Induction)
       A patient scheduled for an elective, planned Cesarean section 
       does not undergo pharmacological labor induction. If both 
       'was_planned_cs' and 'induction' are positive, the induction 
       flag is nullified.

  1.5. Temporal Invariant (Admission vs. Birth Date)
       Chronologically, the admission date must precede or equal the 
       birth date. 

2. DERIVED VARIABLES AND MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS
These checks ensure that derived features mathematically align with 
their source components.

  2.1. HTN Cluster Recalculation (The HTN Cluster)
       The overarching variable 'any_htn' must be the absolute maximum 
       of its components ('chronic_htn', 'gestational_htn', 'preeclampsia'). 
       To enforce structural truth, 'any_htn' is strictly recalculated 
       from these components.

  2.2. BMI Mathematical Verification
       The provided computed BMI is verified against the standard formula: 
       Weight / (Height in meters)^2. Discrepancies larger than 1.0 
       (accounting for minor rounding differences) are treated as math 
       errors and overwritten with the correct calculation.

3. DATA LEAKAGE PREVENTION
Variables containing future information (post-prediction-time) are 
strictly removed to prevent target leakage.

  3.1. Exclusion of Birth Weight ('birth_weight_g')
       Neonatal birth weight is determined post-delivery and is inherently 
       unavailable at the triage/admission decision point. It is completely 
       dropped from the feature space.

  3.2. Note on Meconium and Membrane Color
       While 'meconium' is retained per project guidelines (as it often 
       manifests early in labor), it is acknowledged as a borderline 
       data-leakage variable since membrane rupture may occur intrapartum. 
       This will be documented formally in the Study Limitations section.
